Puzzle &
Summoners

Link. Summon. Conquer.

Puzzle & Summoners is a quick-to-learn, hard-to-master puzzle RPG where you link same-type tiles to unleash powerful attacks. Build a team of heroes, chain skills, and outsmart bosses with smart positioning and elemental synergy.

FEATURES

  • Link-to-attack puzzle combat (not a basic match-3)
  • Summon and collect heroes, each with unique skills and roles
  • Elemental advantages that change every fight
  • Upgrade, evolve, and customize your squad for your playstyle
  • Dungeons, bosses, and limited-time events with new rewards
  • Smooth, bite-sized sessions?perfect for short breaks

HOW IT PLAYS

Link tiles to charge your party.

Time your skill chains for big damage and clutch saves.

Adapt your team to enemy elements and mechanics.

Optional in-app purchases are available. A network connection may be required for events and account features.
